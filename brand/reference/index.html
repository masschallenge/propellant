MassChallenge brand guidelines

How MassChallenge shows up everywhere we make things: web products, websites, Office and Google Workspace files, print, slides, video and social graphics. It covers the name, the logo, color, type, voice, messaging, contact and legal details, the graphic devices, imagery and video.

The standard is the MassChallenge brand guidelines from MassChallenge Marketing. This page applies them to the things Propellant builds, and adds the rules for the signed in products. If this page and the brand guidelines ever disagree, the brand guidelines win; ask MassChallenge Marketing. Most of the interface side is already built into Propellant, so when you use its components and tokens (the named values for color, type and spacing), you follow these guidelines without extra work.

The name

Section titled “The name”
  • Write MassChallenge as one word with a capital M and a capital C.
  • Do not abbreviate it to MC in running text. The MC monogram is a logo, not a word.
  • Do not add a trademark symbol to the name or the logo.

Logo

Section titled “Logo”

The logo is the most recognizable part of the MassChallenge brand, so we treat it with care. Use the approved files or the Logo component, and never redraw, retype or recolor it.

Versions

Section titled “Versions”
Version Use it for Where to get it
Vertical The default for most layouts. From MassChallenge Marketing.
Horizontal Where width is plentiful and height is tight: headers, footers, sponsor slides and banners. It is the version Propellant uses in product headers and sign in pages. assets/logos/masschallenge-logo-horizontal-*.png, <Logo />
MC mark (the monogram) Where the audience already knows us. In Propellant: compact navigation, favicons, app icons and avatars. assets/logos/masschallenge-logo-monogram-*.png, <Logo lockup="monogram" />
Local logos Work tied to one site. US sites use the city and international sites use the country. From MassChallenge Marketing.

Propellant ships the horizontal logo and the MC mark only. The vertical logo and the local logos come from MassChallenge Marketing; never draw or assemble them yourself.

The horizontal logo is the MC mark, a thin vertical divider and the MASSCHALLENGE wordmark. Keep the parts together, at the proportions in the files. Use the MC mark on its own only where the MassChallenge name is already clear from the context.

Local logos. Approved local files exist for Boston, Israel, the UK and Switzerland. Dallas is not produced yet. Use only the approved local files, and never type a location next to the logo.

Treatments

Section titled “Treatments”
Treatment Use it on Files and component
Full color White and Warm White only. *-color.png, <Logo />
White Navy, Black, Deep Teal, Teal, MC Blue, and photographs with enough contrast. *-white.png, <Logo treatment="white" />
Black One color print, and light backgrounds that the full color logo does not suit. *-black.png, <Logo treatment="black" />

A treatment is the color version of the logo you pick for a background. The one color treatments are the same artwork in a single color, so the gap between the M and the C stays open. Each treatment exists for both versions Propellant ships.

  • Never put the white logo on light colors such as Cyan, Yellow or Lime.
  • On a busy photograph, add the hero overlay or a solid color block behind the logo.

Clear space

Section titled “Clear space”

Keep a clear space around the logo equal to the height of the “M” in the MASSCHALLENGE wordmark, on every side. Nothing enters it: no type, images, edges or other logos. In the horizontal files the M is about 40% of the logo’s height, so a horizontal logo shown 32px tall needs about 13px of clear space all around. For the MC mark on its own, take the same measure from the M of the mark.

Align the horizontal logo to a corner or the top edge of its area.

Minimum size

Section titled “Minimum size”
Version Smallest on screen Smallest in print
Vertical 60px wide 20mm wide
Horizontal 160px wide. At Propellant’s default height of 24px it is about 178px wide. Narrower than 160px, use the MC mark instead. 30mm wide
MC mark 16px tall (about 29px wide, above the guide’s 24px wide) 8mm

The Logo component renders at 24px tall by default. Size it with a height class such as h-8; the width follows.

Placement

Section titled “Placement”
  • Place the logo at the start of a header or sidebar, aligned with the content below it.
  • In a product, link the logo to the product’s home.
  • Give the logo an accessible name of MassChallenge. When the name is written right beside it, mark the logo as decorative with <Logo label="" />.

Things to avoid

Section titled “Things to avoid”
  • Do not recolor, stretch, rotate, outline, flip or crop the logo.
  • Do not add shadows, glows, gradients or other effects.
  • Do not rearrange, rebuild or retype the logo, resize its parts or remove the divider.
  • Do not create new lockups or sub-logos outside the sub-brand system.
  • Do not place the full color logo on dark colors or busy photographs; use the white treatment.
  • Do not place the white logo on light colors such as Cyan, Yellow or Lime.
  • Do not place the logo inside a shape or container that crowds its clear space.
  • Do not use the MC mark as a letter in a word or headline.

Using color

Section titled “Using color”
  • One warm accent per piece, and never as a large background.
  • Cyan only on dark backgrounds: Navy, Black and Deep Teal.
  • MC Blue is for the logo and shapes, not text. On light backgrounds, colored text is Teal.
  • Orange takes dark text, never white.
  • Pair every status color with a word or a symbol, never color alone.
  • Text stands out from its background by a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1, and large text and the edges of controls by at least 3:1.

Status colors are separate

Section titled “Status colors are separate”

Propellant’s error, warning and success colors (the magenta, orange and green tokens, such as --critical, --warning and --success) stay for interface status. Raspberry, Orange and Lime are decorative brand accents: never use them to signal status, and never use a status color as decoration.

Charts

Section titled “Charts”

Use the chart colors in this order: Teal, Yellow, Navy, MC Blue, Deep Teal and Gray 300 (#848F92, --gray-300). This order is Propellant’s application of the palette. Label the data directly, on or beside each line, bar or slice, rather than relying on a legend and color alone.

In the signed in products

Section titled “In the signed in products”

The product text styles do not change, and Oswald is never used inside a signed in product.

  • Use sentence case for titles, labels and buttons. Capitals belong only to Oswald, the wordmark and the labels of the graphic devices below.
  • Use the eleven named text styles in Propellant rather than choosing sizes by eye.
  • Use tabular figures (digits of equal width) wherever numbers line up in columns.
  • Keep lines of running text to about 68 characters.

Voice

Section titled “Voice”

MassChallenge sounds bold and ambitious. Our primary line is “We back the rare humans with the grit to solve the toughest challenges,” and we are “a global network turning bold ideas into outcomes that matter.” Our products should sound like the same people: direct, plain spoken and on the side of the founders we serve. In a product the stakes are practical, so keep the confidence and drop the slogans.

What that sounds like:

  • Bold and ambitious. “Rare humans”, “grit”, “toughest challenges”.
  • Founder first. “We back the rare humans with the grit to solve the toughest challenges.”
  • About outcomes and access. “From proof to scale”, “outcomes that matter”, “access to the people and power that move markets”.
  • Short declarative headlines in capitals. On public pages, in Oswald.

“It takes a community to raise a startup.” is not a MassChallenge line. It is a founder’s testimonial. Never present it as a tagline or as our words.

  • Get to the point. Use short sentences and everyday words, the way you would explain it to a colleague.
  • Write as a person, not a system. Use “we” for MassChallenge and “you” for the person reading.
  • Be confident without hype. Skip buzzwords such as leverage, seamless and robust, and skip exclamation marks.
  • Write for the whole community. Use the words the people using the product use: programs, startups, applications, mentors, judges.
  • Use sentence case for every title, label and button in a product.
  • Put a verb on every button that says what happens, such as Save changes or Submit application.
  • When something goes wrong, say what happened and how to fix it. Do not apologize or blame.
  • Use commas, colons and periods. Do not use em dashes or en dashes.

Graphic devices

Section titled “Graphic devices”

These are the standard graphic devices. Build them from the palette and the logo files, and never redraw the logo.

Hero overlay

Section titled “Hero overlay”

A thin linear gradient over a hero photo, from Navy black #051925 to Ocean #037AA6, at 15 to 30% opacity. It runs toward the subject: the side with the text is darker, and the subject’s side stays clear, so warm light still glows through. Use it on heroes, social posts and event graphics. On the web it is the propellant-hero class, or the --mc-hero-overlay-text-side and --mc-hero-overlay-subject-side tokens.

The overlay alone does not make text readable: at 15 to 30% it barely darkens a bright area, so white text over one stays near 2:1. Use it only where the text sits on an area that is already dark and calm, and check that the text still reaches 4.5:1 (3:1 for large headlines). Otherwise set the text on a solid Navy, Black or Deep Teal block.

The full frame teal duotone (#C7F2EF to #003349) is retired, because it reads as clinical. Use the hero overlay instead.

Hexagon network frame

Section titled “Hexagon network frame”

A circular photo crop set inside a network of hexagons and lines. Draw the hexagon shapes in MC Blue and the lines in the light tint #F3FAFC. Use it for a single photo beside text.

Geometric pattern

Section titled “Geometric pattern”

The brand pattern: MC Blue triangles with the light tint #F3FAFC or Warm White, and an Oswald label in capitals on top, such as “ACCELERATOR” or “CHALLENGE”. Use it for covers and headers.

Set the label in Black on the light parts of the pattern, or in White on a solid Navy or Deep Teal panel. Check the contrast of every label: never set White straight on the triangles, because White on MC Blue is only 2.8:1.

Stat callouts

Section titled “Stat callouts”

A large number in Oswald Bold with a short label. Set the number in Teal on light backgrounds and Cyan on dark ones, with the label in Black or White. A single Yellow rule is an optional warm accent. Never use Raspberry for numbers. Use the approved figures, with their as-of date.

Imagery

Video

Section titled “Video”
  • The photography rules apply: real people, balanced exposure and warmth in the frame.
  • Every video uses the standard logo open and end slates and consistent lower thirds. MassChallenge Marketing approves final exports before they go live.
  • Frame interviews on the rule of thirds, with the eyes near a top intersection and lead room in the direction the subject faces.
  • Lower thirds: a Navy block with a Cyan rule. The name in Oswald SemiBold and the title in Lato, bottom left, inside the title safe area.
  • Use only footage MassChallenge has licensed or shot itself.

Sub-brands and partners

Section titled “Sub-brands and partners”

Only MassChallenge Marketing creates sub-brand lockups and endorsement marks. The structures below describe what they look like, so you can recognize and place them; never build one yourself.

Sub-brand lockup. For programs MassChallenge runs under its own name, such as Breakthrough Bio: the MC mark, an MC Blue rule (Cyan on dark), the program name in Oswald SemiBold and the location in tracked Oswald Regular. Partner logos never join a lockup.

Partner initiative endorsement. For programs that partners own and run with MassChallenge guidance: the initiative name above a 1pt rule, and an endorsement line below it (“WITH MASSCHALLENGE” or “WITH PARTNER & MASSCHALLENGE”), aligned left.

Corporate partner lockup. For a challenge program a partner commissions:

  • The partner’s logo first, on the left, then a 1pt vertical rule a little taller than the logos, then the MassChallenge vertical logo on the right.
  • Give both logos equal visual weight. The gap on each side of the rule equals the height of the M in the MC mark.
  • The rule is MC Gray on light backgrounds, and white or light gray on dark ones.
  • Both logos are full color on white, or both white on dark or on the partner’s brand color.
  • Do not put MassChallenge first, mix a full color logo with a white one, let one logo overpower the other, drop the rule or use a plus sign.

Whether a partner’s logo appears alongside ours is set in the partnership agreement.

Iconography

Section titled “Iconography”
  • In brand material, icons are single color line icons, in Teal on light backgrounds and Cyan on dark ones, always beside a word.
  • In products, icons sit beside text: their stroke follows the weight of the text and their size follows its text style.
  • Use one icon per idea across a product.
  • An icon that acts alone, without a visible label, needs an accessible name.

The Propellant mark

Section titled “The Propellant mark”

Propellant has a mark of its own. It stands for the design system and nothing else: its documentation, Storybook (our workshop for trying out components), this repository and the package. It is drawn from the same parts as the rest of the brand. Like the MassChallenge logo, it pairs a gray stem with a teal bowl and sets the name beside a thin divider. It is built from Propellant’s own capsule shapes, and the stem breaks into two fading capsules: the exhaust that gives the system its name.

  • Use the Propellant mark only to refer to the design system. Never use it in place of the MassChallenge logo in a product or website, and never place the two marks so they read as one.
  • The clear space, minimum size and things to avoid for the MassChallenge logo apply to this mark too. Keep the mark and the icon at least 16px tall, and the lockup at least 24px tall.
