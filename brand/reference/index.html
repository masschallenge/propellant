MassChallenge brand guidelines

How MassChallenge shows up in our web products and websites: the name, the logo, color, type and voice. Most of it is already built into Propellant, so when you use its components and tokens (the named values for color, type and spacing), you follow these guidelines without extra work.

The name

Section titled “The name”
  • Write MassChallenge as one word with a capital M and a capital C.
  • Do not abbreviate it to MC in running text. The MC monogram is a logo, not a word.
  • Do not add a trademark symbol in interfaces.

Logo

Section titled “Logo”

The logo is the most recognizable part of the MassChallenge brand, so we treat it with care. Use the files we supply or the Logo component, and never redraw, retype or recolor it.

Lockups

Section titled “Lockups”

A lockup is a fixed arrangement of the logo’s parts. There are two.

Lockup Use it for Files
Horizontal The default. Headers, sign in pages, footers, documents and anywhere the full name should be read. assets/logos/masschallenge-logo-horizontal-*.png
Monogram Small spaces where the horizontal lockup would fall below its minimum size: favicons, app icons, avatars and compact navigation. assets/logos/masschallenge-logo-monogram-*.png

The horizontal lockup is the MC monogram, a thin vertical divider and the MASSCHALLENGE wordmark. Keep the three parts together, at the proportions in the files. Use the monogram on its own only where the MassChallenge name is already clear from the context.

Logo colors

Section titled “Logo colors”
Part Color
M and wordmark Gray #828486
C and divider Teal #17ACBC

These are the colors of the logo files. They belong to the logo only; interface colors come from the palette below.

Treatments

Section titled “Treatments”
Treatment Use it on Files and component
Full color White and light neutral backgrounds, such as the page and content surfaces. *-color.png, <Logo />
White The brand teal, dark surfaces, dark mode and photographs dark enough to keep the logo legible. *-white.png, <Logo treatment="white" />
Black Places where only one dark color is available, such as single color print, and light backgrounds that the full color logo does not suit. *-black.png, <Logo treatment="black" />

A treatment is the color version of the logo you pick for a background. The one color treatments are the same artwork in a single color, so the gap between the M and the C stays open. Each treatment exists for both lockups.

Clear space

Section titled “Clear space”

Give the logo room to breathe. Keep the space around it free of text, images and edges. The clear space on every side is half the height of the logo. A horizontal logo shown 32px tall needs 16px of clear space all around.

Minimum size

Section titled “Minimum size”
Lockup Smallest height on screen
Horizontal 24px. Below this the wordmark stops being readable; use the monogram instead.
Monogram 16px.

The Logo component renders at 24px tall by default. Size it with a height class such as h-8; the width follows.

Placement

Section titled “Placement”
  • Place the logo at the start of a header or sidebar, aligned with the content below it.
  • In a product, link the logo to the product’s home.
  • Give the logo an accessible name of MassChallenge. When the name is written right beside it, mark the logo as decorative with <Logo label="" />.

Things to avoid

Section titled “Things to avoid”
  • Do not change the logo colors or color the monogram and wordmark differently from the files.
  • Do not stretch, squash, rotate, skew or crop the logo.
  • Do not add shadows, glows, outlines, gradients or other effects.
  • Do not rearrange the lockup, resize its parts or remove the divider.
  • Do not place the full color logo on the brand teal, dark backgrounds or busy photographs; use the white treatment.
  • Do not recreate the wordmark in a font or type the name in its place.
  • Do not place the logo inside a shape or container that crowds its clear space.
  • Do not use the monogram as a letter in a word or headline.

Color

Section titled “Color”

Brand palette

Section titled “Brand palette”
Name Hex Role
Deep teal #044451 Brand surfaces, such as the panel of a sign in page
Teal #065E6F The primary interactive color in light mode
Mid teal #068DA4 Supporting accents
Bright teal #0FACBD Accents on dark surfaces
Pale teal #D9F6FA Tinted backgrounds
Mist #F3FBFC The lightest tint
Magenta #BF0261 Destructive actions and errors
Yellow #FFC801 Highlights, such as search matches and new items

Using color

Section titled “Using color”
  • Teal means you can act on something: buttons, links, selected controls and focus. Do not use it for headings, names or numbers.
  • Magenta means something is wrong or cannot be undone. Never use it decoratively.
  • Yellow highlights. Always set dark text on it.
  • Our grays carry a slight teal tint, so they sit comfortably next to the teals.
  • Pair every status color with a word or a symbol, never color alone.
  • Text stands out from its background by a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1, and the edges of controls by at least 3:1, in light and dark themes. Propellant’s semantic tokens (named for their job, not their color) are tuned for this, so in interfaces use the tokens rather than the hex values above.

Typography

Section titled “Typography”
Typeface Use it for
Propellant Sans All interface and body text. It is Lato 2.0 at 400, 500, 600 and 700, plus 400 italic, shipped with Propellant.
Oswald Display headlines on public websites and pages, set in capitals. Never inside signed in applications.
  • Use sentence case for titles, labels and buttons. Capitals belong only to Oswald headlines and the wordmark.
  • Use the eleven named text styles in Propellant rather than choosing sizes by eye.
  • Use tabular figures (digits of equal width) wherever numbers line up in columns.
  • Keep lines of running text to about 68 characters.

Voice

Section titled “Voice”

MassChallenge sounds bold and human. On our website we say “We back the rare humans with the grit to solve the toughest challenges” and “It takes a community to raise a startup.” Our products should sound like the same people: direct, plain spoken and on the side of the community we serve. In a product the stakes are practical, so keep the confidence and drop the slogans.

  • Get to the point. Use short sentences and everyday words, the way you would explain it to a colleague.
  • Write as a person, not a system. Use “we” for MassChallenge and “you” for the person reading.
  • Be confident without hype. Skip buzzwords such as leverage, seamless and robust, and skip exclamation marks.
  • Write for the whole community. Use the words the people using the product use: programs, startups, applications, mentors, judges.
  • Use sentence case for every title, label and button.
  • Put a verb on every button that says what happens, such as Save changes or Submit application.
  • When something goes wrong, say what happened and how to fix it. Do not apologize or blame.
  • Use commas, colons and periods. Do not use em dashes or en dashes.

Do and don’t

Section titled “Do and don’t”
Don’t write Write
Leverage our seamless platform to unlock your startup’s full potential! Find mentors who know your market.
Users must complete all required fields prior to submission. Add your company website to submit your application.
Oops! Something went wrong. We could not save your changes. Check your connection and try again.

Iconography

Section titled “Iconography”
  • Icons sit beside text: their stroke follows the weight of the text and their size follows its text style.
  • Use one icon per idea across a product.
  • An icon that acts alone, without a visible label, needs an accessible name.

The Propellant mark

Section titled “The Propellant mark”

Propellant has a mark of its own. It stands for the design system and nothing else: its documentation, Storybook (our workshop for trying out components), this repository and the package. It is drawn from the same parts as the rest of the brand. Like the MassChallenge logo, it pairs a gray stem with a teal bowl and sets the name beside a thin divider. It is built from Propellant’s own capsule shapes, and the stem breaks into two fading capsules: the exhaust that gives the system its name.

File Use it for
assets/brand/propellant-lockup-color.svg The default, on white and light backgrounds.
assets/brand/propellant-lockup-white.svg Deep teal, dark surfaces and dark mode.
assets/brand/propellant-lockup-black.svg Single color print and light backgrounds the color version does not suit.
assets/brand/propellant-mark-*.svg The mark alone, in the same three treatments, where the name is already clear.
assets/brand/propellant-icon.svg Favicons and app icons: the mark on a deep teal tile with concentric corners.
  • Use the Propellant mark only to refer to the design system. Never use it in place of the MassChallenge logo in a product or website, and never place the two marks so they read as one.
  • The clear space, minimum size and things to avoid for the MassChallenge logo apply to this mark too. Keep the mark and the icon at least 16px tall, and the lockup at least 24px tall.
