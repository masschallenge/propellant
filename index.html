Why we call it Propellant

A rocket can have a brilliant design, but it doesn't leave the pad without propellant. MassChallenge works the same way. Founders bring the big ideas, and we help them take off. We wanted the same lift for our own work, so every team can move fast and still look and sound like one MassChallenge. Propellant is the fuel that makes everything at MassChallenge take off.
