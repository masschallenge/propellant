PowerPoint

This guide covers pitch decks, program updates, workshop slides and event presentations in Microsoft PowerPoint. Read the foundations first: they set the colors, fonts and voice this guide applies.

Start from the template

Section titled “Start from the template”
  1. Install propellant-presentation.potx as described in installing the templates.
  2. In PowerPoint, choose File, then New, then Personal (or Shared), and pick Propellant presentation.
  3. The template opens with eight sample slides that show every layout. Keep the ones you need, delete the rest, and add new slides with Home, then New Slide.

Moving an existing deck to Propellant? Choose Design, then the arrow beside the themes, then Browse for Themes, and pick Propellant.thmx. That switches the colors and fonts. Then reapply each slide’s layout from Home, then Layout.

Slide size

Section titled “Slide size”

Slides are widescreen 16:9, 13.333 by 7.5 inches, PowerPoint’s default. Keep it: it fits every screen and video call. Do not switch to 4:3.

Layouts

Section titled “Layouts”

Always pick a layout instead of drawing your own text boxes. Layouts keep titles in the same place on every slide, and screen readers use the title placeholder to name each slide.

Layout Use it for
Title Slide The first slide. Deep teal, white logo, title in capitals, subtitle in Bright teal.
Title and Content Most slides: a title and up to five bullets, a chart, a table or a photo.
Section Header Dividing a long deck into parts. Deep teal, like the title slide. Also the closing slide.
Two Content Before and after, or two options side by side.
Comparison Two options, each with a short heading.
Title Only A big number, a quote or a diagram you place yourself.
Picture with Caption One photo filling the left half, with a title and caption on the right.
Blank A full bleed photo or video.

Use Deep teal slides only for the title, section breaks and the closing slide. Everything in between is white, so the Deep teal moments stand out.

Titles

Section titled “Titles”
  • Make every title say the takeaway: “Applications grew in every program”, not “Applications”.
  • Keep titles to one line, about 40 characters. The template sets them in Oswald capitals; type them in sentence case and the style does the rest.
  • Give every slide a different title, even if you then hide it. PowerPoint’s accessibility checker flags a missing title as an error.

Text

Section titled “Text”
  • One idea per slide. If you need more than five bullets, split the slide.
  • Keep bullets short, without full stops on single lines, and one level deep where you can.
  • Never shrink text below 18 pt to make it fit. Microsoft recommends 18 pt or larger. Cut words instead.
  • Put detail in the speaker notes, not on the slide.

Big numbers and quotes

Section titled “Big numbers and quotes”

The two Title Only samples show how:

  • A big number: the number in Oswald at about 120 pt in Deep teal, with one line underneath saying what it means and where it comes from. Always give the source.
  • A quote: the quote in Lato at about 36 pt with real curly quote marks, and the person’s name, role and company below in Slate. Only quote people who agreed to be quoted.

Charts

Section titled “Charts”
  • Insert charts with Insert, then Chart, so they take their colors from the theme.
  • Use the chart rules in the foundations: one series in Teal, labels on the chart when there are more, four series at most, one axis.
  • Remove what does not help: gridlines down to a few light ones, no 3D, no shadows, no chart border.
  • Put the takeaway in the slide title and the source in small text below the chart.

Photos and video

Section titled “Photos and video”
  • Use photos that fill the frame: the Picture with Caption layout, or a full bleed photo on a Blank slide.
  • When you crop, crop on purpose: keep faces and heads whole and leave the people looking into the slide. Use Picture Format, then Crop, then Fill, and drag the photo to place it.
  • Put text on a photo only over a calm, dark area, or on a Deep teal band. Use the white logo on dark photos.
  • For video, insert the file or a link with Insert, then Video, add captions, and set it to start when clicked, not automatically.
  • Follow the photography guide, including alt text for every photo.

The logo

Section titled “The logo”

The template places the logo for you: the white logo at the top left of Deep teal slides and the full color logo at the bottom right of white slides. Do not add another logo, and do not move it into the content area. On a full bleed photo slide, the full color logo can disappear into the photo: right click the slide, choose Format Background, check Hide background graphics, and place the white logo from assets/logos in a dark corner instead.

Presenting and sharing

Section titled “Presenting and sharing”
  1. Run Review, then Check Accessibility, and fix every error and warning.
  2. Check the reading order of busy slides in the Reading Order pane.
  3. To send the deck to someone outside MassChallenge, send a PDF, or embed the fonts if they need to edit it.
  4. Before presenting on someone else’s computer, check that Lato and Oswald are installed there, or bring a PDF.

Things to avoid

Section titled “Things to avoid”
  • Animations other than a simple Appear or Fade, and slide transitions other than Fade.
  • Text in Teal, except links, and Teal text on Deep teal.
  • Logos, icons or photos copied from a web search.
  • Busy backgrounds, gradients, shadows and outlines on text.
  • Slides read word for word. If the slide says it all, the presenter has nothing to add.
