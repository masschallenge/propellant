# Graphic devices and print color

## Graphic devices

These are the standard graphic devices. Build them from the palette and the logo files; never
redraw the logo.

| Device                         | What it is                                                                                                                                 | Use it for                                |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------- |
| Geometric pattern              | MC Blue triangles with a scatter of light tint `#F3FAFC` triangles, and an Oswald label in capitals on top, such as ACCELERATOR            | Covers and headers                        |
| Hero overlay                   | A thin linear gradient over a photo from Navy-black `#051925` on the text side to Ocean `#037AA6` toward the subject, at 15 to 30% opacity | Heroes, social posts and event graphics   |
| Hexagon network frame          | A circular photo crop inside a network of MC Blue hexagons joined by lines in the light tint                                               | One photo beside text                     |
| Corporate partner lockup       | The partner logo first, a 1pt vertical rule, then the MassChallenge vertical logo                                                          | A challenge program a partner commissions |
| Sub-brand lockup               | The MC mark, an MC Blue rule (Cyan on dark), the program name in Oswald SemiBold and the location in tracked Oswald Regular                | Programs MassChallenge runs by name       |
| Partner initiative endorsement | The initiative name above a 1pt rule, with WITH MASSCHALLENGE or WITH PARTNER & MASSCHALLENGE below, left aligned                          | Partner programs MassChallenge guides     |
| Stat callouts                  | A large number in Oswald Bold with a short label in capitals                                                                               | One figure that matters                   |
| Program timelines              | Dots on a line, with each step above or below its dot                                                                                      | Program and application dates             |
| Icons                          | Single color line icons with rounded ends, Teal on light, Cyan on dark                                                                     | Labels for areas and steps                |

- Text on the pattern must pass contrast against both colors. Set the label in Black (5.7:1 on MC
  Blue), or in White on a solid Navy or Deep Teal panel. Never White straight on the triangles:
  White on MC Blue is 2.8:1, below 3:1 even for large text.
- The teal duotone is retired. Never wash a photo in teal or any full-frame cool duotone. Use the
  hero overlay, and keep it light enough that warm light glows through.
- Only MassChallenge Marketing creates sub-brand lockups, partner lockups and endorsement marks.
  Describe the structure if asked, but never assemble a lockup yourself, and never draw the
  vertical or local logos. Partner logos never join a sub-brand lockup.
- In a corporate partner lockup the gap on each side of the rule equals the height of the M in the
  MC mark. The rule is MC Gray on light, white or light gray on dark. Both logos full color on
  white, or both white on dark or on the partner's brand color. Never put MassChallenge first, mix
  a full color logo with a white one, let one overpower the other, drop the rule or use a plus
  sign. Whether a partner logo appears beside ours is set in the partnership agreement.
- Stat numbers are Teal on light backgrounds and Cyan on dark ones, with the label in Black or
  White. One Yellow accent rule is optional. Never Raspberry for numbers.
- Timeline dots follow the chart order: Teal, Yellow, Navy, MC Blue, Deep Teal, Gray 300. The words
  beside each dot carry the meaning.
- Icons are always paired with a word.
- One warm accent per piece: Yellow, Orange, Raspberry or Lime, never two together. Raspberry only
  on dark backgrounds; Orange always with dark text.

CMYK values are approximate, so confirm with a printer proof before a large run. Where a color has
a print substitute, use the substitute in print. Ocean `#037AA6` is a gradient stop only and has no
print value of its own. MC Blue and MC Gray belong to the logo artwork and to graphic shapes in MC
Blue; never use them to recolor the logo. The supporting blues and warm accents are reviewed with
each version of the brand guide.
