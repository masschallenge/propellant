# Photography and video

## What to show

- People first: founders, mentors, partners and staff doing something together: working,
  pitching, talking, celebrating.
- Real moments over poses. Posed group shots only for celebrations.
- The whole community: a range of genders, ages, backgrounds and abilities.
- Our own event and program photos before stock.
- Photos with a calm area where a headline or the logo can sit.

## Sources

- Use photos the person provides, MassChallenge's own photos, or licensed stock the person has the
  rights to. Never pull images from a web search or social media.
- Never present a stock photo as a real person, startup, partner or event.
- Never generate images of real, named people. If an image is AI generated, it must not depict a
  real person or event, and the person should know it is generated.
- Avoid clichés: handshakes, people pointing at blank screens, light bulbs, puzzle and chess
  pieces, staged high fives.
- Only feature identifiable people prominently, or name them, if they agreed.

## Treatment

| Treatment     | Use it for                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Natural color | News, event recaps, real people's stories, documentation                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Brand duotone | Big brand moments: a title slide, a cover, a hero. Grayscale photo tinted from Deep teal `#044451` in the shadows to Pale teal `#D9F6FA` in the highlights, optionally fading to solid Deep teal on the side where the headline sits. |

- Never duotone a portrait or a photo where people's identity matters.
- No filters, heavy vignettes or color casts.
- Text and the logo go on a calm area of the photo or on a Deep teal band, never on a busy part.

In HTML, a duotone can be made with a grayscale photo under a Deep teal overlay using
`mix-blend-mode: multiply`, or with an SVG color matrix filter.

## Cropping and size

- Crop on purpose: keep faces and heads whole, never crop at the neck, elbows or knees, and leave
  people facing into the page.
- Never stretch or squash. Keep proportions.
- One strong photo beats a collage.
- Round photo corners 12 px on the web, about 3 mm in documents; full bleed photos stay square.
- Photos people upload, such as profile photos, are shown whole and never auto cropped.

## Video

- Captions on every video. Short: under a minute for social, under three for recaps.
- Strong first frame and thumbnail with people in it.
- An Oswald title on Deep teal at the start and the white logo on Deep teal at the end, not a logo
  on screen throughout.
- Play on click, never autoplay with sound. Licensed music and footage only.

## Alt text

One sentence on what matters in the image, such as "Founders pitching to judges at demo day". Name
people only if they agreed to be featured. Mark decorative images as decorative.
