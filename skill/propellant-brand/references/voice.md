# Voice

MassChallenge sounds bold and human. Our website says "We back the rare humans with the grit to
solve the toughest challenges" and "It takes a community to raise a startup." Everything we write
should sound like the same people: direct, plain spoken and on the side of the community we serve.

## Rules

- **Get to the point.** Lead with the decision, the ask or the takeaway. Short sentences, everyday
  words, the way you would explain it to a colleague.
- **Write as a person.** "We" for MassChallenge, "you" for the reader.
- **Confident without hype.** No buzzwords such as leverage, seamless, robust, synergy, unlock,
  empower or best in class. No exclamation marks.
- **Use the community's words:** programs, startups, founders, applications, mentors, judges,
  partners, cohorts.
- **Sentence case** for every title, heading, label and button. Capitals only in Oswald headlines.
- **Buttons say what happens:** "Save changes", "Submit application", "Download the template".
- **When something goes wrong,** say what happened and how to fix it, without apologizing or
  blaming.
- **Punctuation:** commas, colons and periods. Never em dashes or en dashes; rewrite the sentence
  instead. Use real quotation marks and apostrophes.
- **Facts:** never invent statistics, quotes, names, dates or results. Use what the person gave
  you, cite sources, and say plainly when something is not known.

## Headlines

Big Oswald headlines can be bolder than body text: short, declarative, and about people and
outcomes. Examples from our site: "Born in Massachusetts. Built for the world." "Your first
believer." Keep them under about eight words.

## Do and don't

| Don't write                                                             | Write                                                                |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Leverage our seamless platform to unlock your startup's full potential! | Find mentors who know your market.                                   |
| Users must complete all required fields prior to submission.            | Add your company website to submit your application.                 |
| Oops! Something went wrong.                                             | We could not save your changes. Check your connection and try again. |
| Q3 Update                                                               | Applications grew in every program this quarter                      |
