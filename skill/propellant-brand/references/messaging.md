# Messaging, contact and legal

Everything here is quoted from masschallenge.org as read on 2026-11-11. Use it word for word. Do
not invent other lines, figures, names or addresses.

## Lines

- **Primary line** (home hero, https://masschallenge.org/): "We back the rare humans with the grit
  to solve the toughest challenges."
- **Positioning** (About page, https://masschallenge.org/about-us/): "Born in Massachusetts. Built
  for the world." and "A global network turning bold ideas into outcomes that matter."
- "It takes a community to raise a startup." is a founder testimonial, not a MassChallenge line.
  Never use it as a tagline.

## Boilerplate

Quoted from the About page, with its dashes replaced by commas. Use the shortest that fits.

- **Short:** Born in Massachusetts and built for the world, MassChallenge backs the rare humans with
  the grit to solve the world's hardest challenges.
- **Medium:** the short version, then: What began in New England's innovation corridor is now a
  global network connecting startups, experts, and partners across industries and continents, all
  working to turn bold ideas into outcomes that matter.
- **Long:** the medium version, then: We are the earliest believer in founders tackling complex,
  regulated, and high-stakes problems, providing the infrastructure, access, and catalytic capital
  to move from proof to scale. Since 2009, MassChallenge has supported more than 5,000 startups
  across five Challenge Areas, helping them raise $16B+ in funding, create 90K jobs, and
  collaborate with more than 500 partners worldwide.

## Names

- **Challenge Areas:** Healthcare and Life Sciences; Security and Resiliency; Sustainable Food
  Systems; Climate; Global Financial Systems. Write "and" in running text; "&" only in tight labels.
- **Accelerators:** Traction, Challenge, Breakthrough Bio. Corporate Partners is the partnership
  offer.
- **Audiences:** Founders, Experts, Collaborators. In products, keep the words people use there:
  startups, applications, mentors, judges, programs.
