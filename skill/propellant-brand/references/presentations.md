## Start from the template

Open `assets/templates/propellant-presentation.pptx`. It is 16:9 (13.333 by 7.5 inches) and has
the Propellant theme (Oswald headings, Lato body, the brand colors), the logo on every layout, and
eight sample slides showing each layout. Delete the sample slides you do not need and add slides
from the template's layouts. Never draw your own title boxes or change the slide size.

## The layouts

The layouts are in this order in the slide master (index from 0):

| Index | Layout               | Use it for                                                              |
| ----- | -------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 0     | Title Slide          | The first slide. Deep teal with the white logo.                         |
| 1     | Title and Content    | Most slides: a title and up to five bullets, or a chart, table or photo |
| 2     | Section Header       | Section breaks and the closing slide. Deep teal.                        |
| 3     | Two Content          | Two things side by side                                                 |
| 4     | Comparison           | Two options, each with a short heading                                  |
| 5     | Title Only           | A big number, a quote or a diagram you place yourself                   |
| 6     | Blank                | A full bleed photo                                                      |
| 7     | Content with Caption | A chart or table with an explanation beside it                          |
| 8     | Picture with Caption | One photo on the left half, title and caption on the right              |

Use Deep teal layouts only for the title, section breaks and the closing slide.

## Slide content

- Every slide has a unique title in the title placeholder. Write the takeaway, not the topic:
  "Applications grew in every program", not "Applications". Type titles in sentence case: the
  theme shows them in Oswald capitals.
- One idea per slide, at most five bullets, one level deep where possible.
- Never below 18 pt. Body text is 24 pt at the first level and 20 pt at the second. Cut words
  rather than shrinking text.
- Put detail in the speaker notes.
- Text in Ink on white slides, White on Deep teal. Teal only for links.
- No animations beyond Appear or Fade, no transitions beyond Fade, no shadows, outlines,
  gradients, 3D or clip art.
- A typical deck: title slide, an agenda or the point in one slide, sections with a Section Header
  each, and a closing slide with the next step and a contact.

## Big numbers, quotes and photos

- **Big number:** on Title Only, the number in Oswald (use the theme heading font) at about 120 pt
  in Deep teal `#044451`, with one Lato line below at about 22 pt in Slate `#4B575B` saying what it
  means and its source. Only use numbers the person gave you or that come from a cited source.
  Never invent statistics.
- **Quote:** on Title Only, the quote in Lato at about 36 pt in Ink with curly quote marks, and the
  name, role and company below at 18 pt in Slate. Only real quotes from people who agreed.
- **Photo:** Picture with Caption, or a full bleed photo on Blank. Follow
  [photography.md](photography.md). On a dark full bleed photo, hide the background graphics and
  place the white logo in a dark corner.
- **Charts:** native charts so they use the theme colors; follow [charts.md](charts.md).

## Google Slides

Google Slides opens the same `.pptx`. Tell the person to upload it to Drive and choose **File**,
then **Save as Google Slides**, and to add Lato and Oswald from **More fonts** in the font menu if
they are missing. To rebrand an existing deck, **Slide**, then **Change theme**, then **Import
theme**, and pick the Propellant presentation.
