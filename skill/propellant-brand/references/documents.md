## Start from the template

Open `assets/templates/propellant-document.docx`. It has US Letter pages with one inch margins,
the logo in the header, "MassChallenge | Page n" in the footer, the Propellant theme and every
style below, plus sample content. Replace the sample content and keep the styles. Never build the
document on a blank file.

This template replaces any other default document style: it applies the MassChallenge brand
guide, which MassChallenge Marketing owns.

## Structure

1. **Title** in the Title style: Oswald SemiBold, 32 pt, Teal, shown in capitals by the style.
2. **Subtitle** (optional) in the Subtitle style: the program, period or audience.
3. **Status line** in italics, such as "Status: Draft", and "Author: name" on the line below.
4. **Overview** as Heading 1, with one or two paragraphs that give the point, the ask or the
   decision.
5. **Sections** as Heading 1, with Heading 2 for parts inside them.

Keep documents to about four pages unless the person asks for more. Move detail to an appendix.

## Styles

Apply styles; never format text by hand to look like a heading.

| Style                    | Font                      | Size                    | Color           |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------- | ----------------------- | --------------- |
| Title                    | Oswald SemiBold, capitals | 32 pt                   | Teal `#00708E`  |
| Subtitle                 | Lato                      | 14 pt                   | Black `#222222` |
| Heading 1                | Lato Bold                 | 20 pt                   | Teal            |
| Heading 2                | Lato Bold                 | 15 pt                   | Black           |
| Heading 3                | Lato Bold                 | 12.5 pt                 | Black           |
| Normal                   | Lato                      | 11 pt, line spacing 1.5 | Black           |
| List Bullet, List Number | Lato                      | 11 pt                   | Black           |

Headings are sentence case. Use real list styles, never typed dashes. Colored text is always Teal.
Where Lato or Oswald will not load, Aptos Narrow Bold in capitals stands in for the title and
Aptos for the text. Never Arial. If the template's styles differ from this table, follow the
template.

## Tables, callouts and images

- **Tables:** header row with a Navy `#10263F` fill and White bold text, thin Gray 100 `#DBE1E3`
  rules between rows, Warm White `#F8F8F5` on alternate rows, no vertical borders. Set the header
  row to repeat. Left align text, right align numbers. No merged cells. The template's sample table
  shows the formatting; copy it.
- **Callout:** one paragraph with a Warm White `#F8F8F5` fill and a Teal `#00708E` left border, as
  in the template. At most one per page.
- **Images:** in line with text, full or half text width, with alt text and a caption when context
  helps. Follow [photography.md](photography.md).
- **Links:** Teal and underlined, with text that says where they go.

## Google Docs

Google Docs opens the same `.docx`. Tell the person to upload it to Drive and choose **File**, then
**Save as Google Docs**. Lato and Oswald are available from **More fonts** in the font menu.
Google Docs cannot set capitals by style, so type the title in capitals there.

Recommend a PDF for readers outside MassChallenge, since they may not have the fonts.
