Propellant is the MassChallenge design system. It is the shared kit our teams use to build every
MassChallenge website and product: our colors and fonts, ready made pieces like buttons and forms,
and the guidance for putting them together. When everyone builds from the same parts, people get
the same clear, accessible experience wherever they meet us.

## How it is organized

Propellant has one set of foundations and one brand, and a version for each platform we build on.

- **Shared by every platform:** the [brand](/brand/), the [guidelines](/guidelines/) and the way
  we [contribute](/contributing/).
- **One version per platform:** each lives in its own repository and has its own releases. The
  [web version](/web/) is available today. Versions for [iOS](/platforms/ios/),
  [Android](/platforms/android/) and [Office](/platforms/office/) are planned.

## Where this site comes from

This site lives in [`masschallenge/propellant`](https://github.com/masschallenge/propellant). The
pages for each platform come from that platform's repository, taken from its latest release, so
what you read here always matches what teams can install. To change a platform page, use **Edit
page** at the bottom of it, which opens the file in the right repository.
